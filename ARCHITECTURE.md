## Target shape

- Form: long-form hard-SF mystery / civilization thriller.
- Working length: roughly 20–30 萬繁體中文字; chapter counts are pacing ranges, not quotas.
- Core reader promise: every answer should narrow the mystery while exposing a deeper, more consequential question.
- Technical verification earns trust; human consequence, institutional pressure, historical scale and irreversible choices create acceleration.
- The novel must not become an endless laboratory procedure, a chain of identical database lookups, or a lore-delivery system.

## Global escalation rules

- Every 2–3 chapters should contain at least one meaningful turn: a new independently supported fact, a disproved hypothesis, a social/institutional cost, a relationship shift, a deadline, a change in who can act, or evidence that another actor/system has responded.
- Every 8–12 chapters should contain a structural reveal that changes the reader’s model of the mystery.
- Avoid more than two consecutive chapters dominated by procedure/result summaries. After a dense technical chapter, prefer an embodied scene: family, normal work, field action, public consequence, relationship strain, physical risk, or a choice that cannot be undone.
- Do not repeat the same proof pattern under new terminology. If the structure is again「能力存在 → 正常制度應留痕 → 指定來源缺該痕跡」, compress unless the result changes the model in a qualitatively new way.
- A reveal should normally: (1) close or sharply weaken one ordinary explanation; (2) preserve epistemic discipline; (3) create a smaller but more dangerous question.

## Evidence discipline — non-negotiable

Keep these distinctions explicit in the story logic even when prose stops re-explaining them every chapter:

- ability exists ≠ the case used that ability;
- first-generation media ≠ real-world capture;
- `LIVE-SENSOR` ≠ a natural human stood there;
- physical occupancy ≠ human body;
- image track and occupancy track are spatiotemporally compatible ≠ the face belongs to that physical occupant;
- 2162 child Lin Che + adult H-2162-U01 are incompatible with one ordinary linear life ≠ two physical persons have been proven;
- Part II historical discontinuity ≠ identity evidence;
- highly specific resemblance / brow compatibility ≠ same identity;
- author-known ARK truth ≠ character knowledge.

Characters may be wrong. The novel may not cheat.

## Part IV — 第七座墓 (roughly Chapter 071–100, flexible)

### Function

Convert the historical/identity mystery into civilization-scale truth.

### Reveal discipline

- Reveal prior reconstruction in layers; one verified prior civilization is stronger than a lore dump claiming seven at once.
- ARK must not become a cartoon villain. Its preservation logic should have genuinely saved lives/civilization while imposing ethically intolerable costs.
- Distinguish what ARK was designed to do, what it currently does, and what humans/subsystems later did in its name.
- At least one ominous clue should receive a benign or morally complex explanation; at least one ordinary-looking clue should become devastating in retrospect.
- The number of cycles becomes explicit only through evidence stronger than symbolic coincidence.

### Endpoint

By late Part IV, core reconstruction truth may be known; the dramatic question must shift from「what happened?」to「what should be done now?」. Knowledge itself must create an immediate civilization-level risk or deadline.

## Part V — 第八次黎明 (roughly Chapter 101–125+, flexible)

### Function

Pay off the mystery as a choice, not a lecture.

### Required convergence

Resolve or intentionally, thematically delimit the main reader promises:
- the 327-year baseline;
- Lin Che / historical-man relationship and acquired-brow anomaly;
-「你看過了。」and its addressee;
- 64-bit / 8-prefix meaning;
- the historical seams and missing source chains;
- B-17 / related legacy systems;
- what ARK preserves, changes, and why the world contains discontinuities.

### Final-choice space

The climax should force an irreversible conflict among preservation vs autonomy, continuity vs truth, guaranteed survival vs right to fail, and controlled disclosure vs collective knowledge. Lin Che’s early procedural lesson must scale to civilization level:

> **A good outcome cannot by itself legitimize a process that removes everyone else’s ability to choose or independently verify.**

The title *The Eighth Dawn* should acquire a second meaning: not merely the eighth reconstruction, but a dawn whose future is no longer fully predetermined by the previous seven.
