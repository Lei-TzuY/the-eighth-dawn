# ARCHITECTURE — Full-novel macro plan

> Status: Architecture Checkpoint 002 completed after Chapter 029. Part I〈錯誤年代〉is complete; Part II〈被修補的歷史〉is active from Chapter 030. This file controls macro pacing; `CONTINUITY.md` controls established facts. If they conflict, established continuity wins unless explicitly revised.

## Target shape

- Form: long-form hard-SF mystery / civilization thriller.
- Working length: roughly 20–30 萬繁體中文字; chapter counts below are pacing ranges, not hard quotas.
- Core reader promise: every answer should narrow the mystery while exposing a deeper, more consequential question.
- The novel must not become an endless laboratory procedure. Technical verification earns trust; human consequence, institutional pressure, historical scale and irreversible choices provide acceleration.

## Global escalation rule

Each 2–3 chapters should contain at least one meaningful turn: a new independently supported fact, a disproved hypothesis, a social/institutional cost, a deadline, a change in who can act, or evidence that another actor/system has responded.

Each 8–12 chapters should contain a major structural reveal that changes the reader’s model of the mystery.

Avoid more than two consecutive chapters dominated by procedure/result summaries. After a dense technical chapter, prefer an embodied scene: field work, confrontation, relationship strain, public consequence, physical risk, or a choice that cannot be undone.

A reveal should normally do three things:
1. close or sharply weaken one ordinary explanation;
2. preserve epistemic discipline — do not jump beyond the evidence;
3. create a smaller but more dangerous question.

## Part II — 被修補的歷史 (Chapter 030 → roughly 055) — active

### Function

Expand the mystery from one case to the historical infrastructure of the city. The question changes from “what is this file?” to “why does normal history repeatedly contain compatible seams?”

### Opening false-resolution arc

For the first several Part II chapters:

- Treat “post-2164 fabrication using consolidated municipal historical data” as the strongest ordinary hypothesis.
- Let it explain as much as it honestly can: access to historical geometry, equipment catalog pointers, legacy-ingest background and early municipal media series.
- Preserve the independent nature of the 2162 original calibration witness card: Chapter 030 establishes that the original card did **not** migrate into the 2164 municipal baseline repository; only secondary equipment index / calibration-status information did.
- Do not immediately break the mundane hypothesis. First determine whether ordinary later access, secondary copies, catalog permissions or equipment records could truly supply the necessary ingredients.
- When the false resolution eventually fails, one independent fact should break a specific required premise rather than the narrator simply declaring the explanation inadequate.

### Escalation

- Move beyond a single room and a single agency.
- Introduce at most one or two new recurring characters at a time; they need independent goals, not just exposition roles.
- Make institutional resistance heterogeneous: some people protect procedure, some careers, some public stability, some genuinely believe the ordinary explanation.
- Let Lin Che’s credibility, employment and relationships matter. Mystery progress must have a cost.
- Use the current false-resolution arc before escalating toward a historical-system discontinuity.
- Avoid returning to endless micro-forensic analysis of the original sealed medium.

### Major reveal target

By the end of Part II, establish that at least one discontinuity belongs to the historical system itself rather than only to the original medium. The evidence may suggest imported baselines, reconstructed records, duplicated urban history, or an impossible break in provenance — but still must not hand the characters the full civilization-cycle answer.

### Part-II endpoint

The case becomes a history problem, not merely a media-forensics problem.

## Part III — 沒有前史的人 (roughly Chapter 056–080)

### Function

Bring the identity mystery to the foreground. The city’s discontinuity and Lin Che’s impossible resemblance begin to look like two aspects of the same problem without permitting an early “clone/reconstruction” conclusion.

### Escalation

- Revisit the 2162 unnamed cluster only when new independent evidence changes what can be asked.
- Force Lin Che to choose between personal truth and evidentiary cleanliness at least once; his Chapter 026 lesson should be tested, not merely remembered.
- Let a close relationship challenge the premise that a perfectly documented biography necessarily proves a continuous self.
- The brow feature should receive a payoff or decisive reframing in this part; do not leave it as perpetual atmospheric evidence.
- The 64-bit / 8-prefix mystery may become structurally meaningful here only if evidence earns it. Never infer “eight civilizations” from the number eight alone.

### Mid/late-Part reveal target

Allow the first reliable appearance of a legacy name, identifier, or system concept that can eventually lead to ARK. The string “ARK” may appear near the end of this part if earned, but its meaning should initially be ambiguous and operational rather than cosmological.

### Part-III endpoint

Lin Che can no longer treat his own identity as incidental to the case.

## Part IV — 第七座墓 (roughly Chapter 081–105)

### Function

Convert the historical/identity mystery into the civilization-scale truth.

### Reveal discipline

- Reveal prior reconstruction in layers. One verified prior civilization is more powerful than a lore dump claiming seven at once.
- ARK must not become a cartoon villain. Its preservation logic should have saved real lives/civilization while also imposing an intolerable cost.
- Distinguish what ARK was designed to do, what it currently does, and what later subsystems/humans have done in its name.
- At least one earlier “ominous” clue should receive a benign or morally complex explanation; at least one ordinary-looking clue should become devastating in retrospect.
- The number of cycles should only become explicit when supported by evidence stronger than symbolic coincidence.

### Major reveal target

By late Part IV, the characters and reader may know the core hidden truth: civilization has ended and been reconstructed repeatedly, the current society is the eighth iteration, and ARK is central to preservation/reconstruction.

The remaining question must then change from “what happened?” to “what should be done now?”

### Part-IV endpoint

Knowledge of the truth itself creates an immediate civilization-level risk or deadline.

## Part V — 第八次黎明 (roughly Chapter 106–125+, flexible)

### Function

Pay off the mystery as a choice, not a lecture.

### Required convergence

Bring the major promises into one causal picture:
- why the 327-year baseline exists;
- why Lin Che resembles the historical man and how the acquired brow anomaly is possible;
- what “你看過了。” was and whom it addressed;
- what the 64-bit / 8-prefix structure actually represents;
- why the relevant policy/source chains were incomplete yet often procedurally normal;
- what B-17 and related legacy systems were really doing;
- what ARK preserves, what it changes, and why history contains seams.

Not every minor archival detail needs an explosive answer, but every *reader-promised* mystery must receive a fair payoff or an intentional, thematically meaningful ambiguity.

### Final-choice space

Do not lock the exact ending yet. The climax should force an irreversible choice among values such as:
- preservation versus autonomy;
- continuity versus truth;
- guaranteed survival versus the right to fail;
- controlled disclosure versus civilization-wide knowledge;
- preserving ARK, changing its mandate, distributing its authority, or refusing another unilateral reset.

The climax should make Lin Che’s early procedural lesson matter at civilization scale: **a good outcome cannot by itself legitimize a process that removes everyone else’s ability to choose or independently verify.**

### Ending requirement

Resolve the central moral decision and the main identity/cycle mystery. The final pages may leave the future uncertain, but must not feel like “Part VI begins immediately.” The title *The Eighth Dawn* should acquire a second meaning by the end: not merely the eighth reconstruction, but a dawn whose future is finally not fully predetermined by the previous seven.
