## Commercial drafting rules

- Default chapter target: 1,800–3,500 Traditional Chinese characters, but shorter is preferred to padding.
- Every scene needs an active scene-owner desire, resistance, a turn, a cost, and an exit hook.
- Technical reasoning should ride inside field action, confrontation, failure, relationship pressure, or a physically specific place whenever possible.
- Avoid the default grammar `terminal → table → scope → result → explanation → home`.
- Prefer paying existing reader promises over adding unrelated mystery boxes.
- A mystery-bearing turn should change the reader's model, not merely rename the same boundary.
- Do not reopen held evidence paths without a new independent source or a genuinely different necessity.
