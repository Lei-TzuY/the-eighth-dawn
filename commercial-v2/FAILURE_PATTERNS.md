# Commercial V2 Failure Patterns

Use this as a stop-list during planning and judging. A chapter can be technically correct and still fail commercially.

## F1 — Terminal-table-scope-result-explanation-home

Failure: the protagonist receives a task, reads a terminal, narrows scope, gets a finalized result, explains it, then goes home. Repeating this grammar kills suspense even when the evidence is good.

Correction: put reasoning inside movement, argument, malfunction, field access, deadline, social exposure or an irreversible choice. Change who wants what before changing terminology.

## F2 — Passive protagonist

Failure: Lin Che waits for an independent team to finish and is merely handed the answer.

Correction: preserve the firewall but give the scene-owner an active goal that matters now—protect a person, complete a field job, stop an improper action, secure a lawful witness, make a costly choice, or confront someone whose goal conflicts with his.

## F3 — Procedure cosplay as plot

Failure: another retention review, permission review, publication review, staffing rule or workspace-governance scene after Chapters 092–095.

Correction: that consequence block is closed. The next slice must change scene grammar and preferably change the mystery model, a relationship, a risk or the characters' ability to act.

## F4 — Mystery-box inflation

Failure: introduce a fresh acronym, secret institution, hidden archive, strange stranger or unexplained device simply to create motion.

Correction: reclaim established promises first: the shared anchor, MUNI lineage, 327-year structure, 2158/2160 inconsistency, B-17, the brow detail, or a legitimate prior-civilization trace.

## F5 — Vocabulary-as-evidence

Failure: infer specific lineage because two things share `MUNI`, `continuity`, `legacy`, `anchor`, `archive`, or another suggestive label.

Correction: require signed relation, provenance, independently preserved pointer, physical witness or another direct bridge appropriate to the claim.

## F6 — Evidence escalation by emotion

Failure: because Lin Che is frightened, angry, desperate or personally involved, the narrative quietly treats resemblance, physical occupancy, candidate relation or opaque anchor as stronger evidence.

Correction: emotion may change choices and conflict, never the evidentiary weight.

## F7 — Omniscient institution

Failure: `市政連續性服務` or `MUNI-LEGACY/INGEST` turns into an all-knowing hidden master system because it appears in multiple places.

Correction: keep duties local until direct evidence connects them. Shared institutional node does not establish shared cause, content source or ARK.

## F8 — Tutorial dialogue

Failure: characters politely ask each other questions whose only function is teaching the reader a schema or policy.

Correction: explanation must be contested, needed for an immediate action, or reveal a character priority. If everyone agrees and nothing is at stake, compress it.

## F9 — Institutional NPC cast

Failure: 許芮安 asks the convenient beginner question, 周映真 reads rules, 葉靜宜 dispenses maternal wisdom, then they disappear.

Correction: each recurring character needs independent wants and leverage. They should sometimes be right for reasons Lin Che dislikes, wrong in ways that cost them, or choose something that changes his available path.

## F10 — Competence without danger

Failure: everyone follows evidence discipline perfectly, every process works, and the only tension is whether a field is interpreted correctly.

Correction: let competent people collide over legitimate but incompatible goals. Mistakes are allowed. The story must not cheat, but characters can misjudge urgency, trust the wrong ordinary model, hesitate, overreach or fail socially.

## F11 — Fake deadline

Failure: countdowns or alarms that create urgency but cannot materially change evidence, access, safety, relationships or future options.

Correction: if a deadline appears, missing it must have a bounded, understandable cost. Chapter 095 already used a resource countdown to distinguish convenience from evidence; do not immediately repeat that lesson.

## F12 — Premature cosmology

Failure: jump from the current generic handoff bridge to ARK, seven civilization cycles, clones, reconstruction or time travel.

Correction: first earn one prior-civilization/reconstruction-level trace that ordinary models cannot absorb. Then widen.

## F13 — Grim lore replacing YA propulsion

Failure: dense institutional history with no visceral scene, social friction, wonder, fear, attraction, rivalry, embarrassment, loyalty or choice.

Correction: the high concept has to happen to people in places a camera can see. Keep the intellectual puzzle, but attach it to bodies, relationships and irreversible moments.

## F14 — Ending a chapter after the explanation

Failure: result arrives, everyone understands it, chapter closes on a calm summary.

Correction: exit on a changed problem, a demanded choice, a newly dangerous implication, a conflict, or a concrete next image the reader wants to follow.
